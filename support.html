Keep your run going.

Prototype controls

ActionKeyboardController mapping
MoveA / D or arrow keysLeft stick or D-pad
JumpSpace or XSouth face button
FireZ, J, or CtrlRight trigger
Dash / deflectShift or CEast face button
PauseEscape or PStart

On-screen buttons support pointer input and are intended for touch. Physical controller and iPhone multi-touch compatibility still need device testing; these mappings are not a verified accessory list.

If you fall or lose your health

The prototype returns you to the start or your activated checkpoint. Dash through gold shots to send them back. The dash must recharge before you can use it again.

If the game pauses

Changing focus pauses gameplay. Select Resume when you return. Sound and reduced-effects preferences are available in the menu.

Progress and purchases

The opening-level checkpoint now saves locally on this device. Choose Continue Checkpoint on the title screen to resume with full health; enemies reset when continuing. Completing the level clears that checkpoint and keeps a local completion record. Expanded campaign saves and Full Adventure purchases are still being developed. Do not uninstall a future release to troubleshoot saves without checking the updated support instructions first.

Report a problem or suggest an idea

Include your device, operating system, input method, what you did, and what happened. A screenshot of the game can help.

Open a support issue

GitHub issues are public and require a GitHub account to post. Keep personal information, purchase receipts, account details, and unrelated screenshots out of reports.
