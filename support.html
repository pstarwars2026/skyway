Keep your run going.

Skyway is in development. Chapter One is free. Full Adventure is an optional one-time purchase unlocking Chapters Two and Three. No ads or subscription.

Controls

On touch screens, use the arrows to move and JUMP, FIRE and DASH to act.

ActionKeyboard
MoveA / D or arrow keys
JumpSpace or X
FireZ, J or Ctrl
Dash / deflectShift or C
PauseEscape or P

Checkpoints and saves

Reach the flag to save your checkpoint on this device. Continue with full health; enemies reset. Progress does not sync between devices, and uninstalling can remove your save.

Dash through gold shots to send them back. Sound and reduced-effects options are in the menu.

Purchases

Open CHAPTERS to unlock Full Adventure or use Restore Purchases. Restore with the Apple Account that made the purchase. If Apple is unavailable, try again when connected. A cancelled purchase does not unlock paid chapters.
