The skyway is taking shape.

V2 has reached a private playable prototype. It is not a finished game or an App Store release. The game is called Skyway, with The Last Signal as its subtitle. The mobile campaign is now planned to be free and ad-supported; the release date is not yet final.

Working in the prototype

  • An original 2.5D opening level with three gaps, sentries, a checkpoint, and the Gatekeeper boss.
  • Running, jumping, blaster fire, and a dash that reflects hostile projectiles.
  • Health recovery, a locally saved checkpoint and completion record, pause/resume, keyboard controls, and on-screen buttons.
  • A rigged human courier with idle, run, jump and dash animations, courier-sized mechanical sentries with readable faces, solid bodies, knockback and attack warnings, a distinct Gatekeeper, detailed city scenery and a compact interface.
  • A local Mac prototype and an iPhone/iPad project being tested. The latest art pass has passed fifteen editor gameplay checks; device validation is still pending.

Before the release

  • Finish art, music and sound review, combat tuning, and accessibility.
  • Build and test the remaining campaign and its progression.
  • Validate actual iPhone/iPad touch, controllers, and performance.
  • Complete publisher setup, privacy controls and real-device advertising validation.
  • Complete store materials, signed builds, review, and release.

Free, with advertising on mobile

The planned iPhone/iPad campaign will be free, replacing the earlier paid-unlock proposal. Advertising is being prepared for natural breaks between adventures; no live ads are enabled yet. The native Mac edition is planned to be free and ad-free, with its final chapter selection still undecided. Running the iPad edition on Apple Silicon Macs, including its ads, requires separate validation.

What happens to v1.3?

The existing v1.3 browser-game source remains open source under its existing license. V2 source stays private. This separate public repository contains product information, support, and privacy details only.

No App Store download yet.
