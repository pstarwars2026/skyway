The Last Signal

Leap across forgotten sky stations. Dash through incoming fire and send it back. Reach the beacon beyond the Gatekeeper.

Planned for iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

See development statusControls & feedback

A private playable prototype is ready. The App Store release, final art, and expanded campaign are still being developed.

Find your footing.

Run and jump between generous landing zones. A checkpoint gives you a place to recover when a run goes wrong.

Send it back.

Time a dash through a gold projectile to reflect it. Use your blaster when you need a little more space.

Wake the beacon.

Get past the sentries and take on the Gatekeeper. The opening chapter ends with a complete boss encounter.

The planned release model

A real first adventure.
Free to begin.

One free opening chapter, with a one-time purchase for the remaining campaign. No subscription is planned.

Purchases are not available yet. Full Adventure is planned at US $1.99 once. Local App Store pricing and the final paid content list will be shown before release.

Free opening chapter

Full Adventure
