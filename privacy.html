Privacy, plainly.

The game

Chapter One has no advertising, purchases or game account. Checkpoints, completion records, sound and effects preferences are stored on your device. There is no cloud save; uninstalling can remove local progress.

We do not collect data from the game. The release contains no advertising SDK, and Unity analytics and device statistics are disabled. This policy covers Chapter One; any future change will be described here.

This website

We add no analytics, ads or tracking cookies. Gameplay media is hosted with this page. GitHub Pages may process connection information under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Support

Support issues are public and handled by GitHub. Do not include private information.
