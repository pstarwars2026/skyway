Privacy, plainly.

The game

Chapter One is free. Full Adventure is an optional one-time Apple purchase. There are no ads or game accounts. Checkpoints, completion records, sound and effects preferences are stored on your device. There is no cloud save; uninstalling can remove local progress.

Apple processes purchases and restores through your Apple Account. We do not receive your payment card details. Purchase ownership can be restored; local game progress does not transfer with it.

Purchase service data

The upcoming Full Adventure version uses Unity In-App Purchasing. This service sends device identifiers, device and app information, country or region, session activity, and purchase events to Unity for purchase functionality and analytics. These records can be linked through device identifiers. The game has no advertising SDK and does not request the advertising identifier or use data for advertising tracking.

See Unity's purchasing privacy information and Unity's privacy policy for its data handling and privacy request options. Apple handles your account and payment information under Apple's privacy policy.

This website

We add no analytics, ads or tracking cookies. Gameplay media is hosted with this page. GitHub Pages may process connection information under the GitHub Privacy Statement.
