# Skyway: The Last Signal — in development

[Product preview](https://pstarwars2026.github.io/skyway/) · [Development status](https://pstarwars2026.github.io/skyway/status.html) · [Support](https://pstarwars2026.github.io/skyway/support.html) · [Privacy](https://pstarwars2026.github.io/skyway/privacy.html)

A new 2.5D action adventure planned for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Run the forgotten skyway, jump between platforms, and time a dash to reflect hostile fire. The private prototype includes an opening level, checkpoint, sentries, and a boss.

**In development. Not yet available on the App Store.** The release date and final paid content list are not yet settled. Full Adventure is planned at US $1.99 as a one-time purchase; local App Store prices may differ. The screenshot shows actual prototype gameplay with unfinished art.

![Actual prototype gameplay; unfinished art](assets/prototype-gameplay.png)

## Planned free and paid gameplay

| Free opening chapter | Full Adventure — planned |
|---|---|
| A complete level and boss | Remaining campaign, still in development |
| Replay without a timer or energy limit | One-time non-consumable purchase |
| Run, jump, fire, and deflect | Shared iPhone/iPad/Mac ownership intended, pending validation |

No purchases are currently available. Final artwork, additional chapters, expanded campaign progression, real-device testing, and purchase/restore validation remain before release.
